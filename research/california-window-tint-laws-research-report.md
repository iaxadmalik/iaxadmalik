### Legal Terms and Plain English Pairs

- VLT (Visible Light Transmittance) plain definition: the percentage of outside light that passes through the glass and film together; a higher number means a lighter window.
- Glazing plain definition: the glass itself, including any factory tint built into it.
- Combined VLT plain definition: the total light that gets through the factory glass and the added film measured together, which is what an officer's meter reads.
- AS-1 line plain definition: a mark near the top of many windshields showing how far down tinting material is allowed.
- Luminous transmittance plain definition: another way of measuring how much light passes through a screen or film.
- Correctable violation (fix-it ticket) plain definition: a citation you can clear by fixing the problem and showing proof, usually for a small fee, often without points.
- Certificate of correction plain definition: the signed proof that you fixed the violation, submitted to the court.
- FMVSS 205 plain definition: the federal safety standard for vehicle glass that the front side window film and glass must meet.

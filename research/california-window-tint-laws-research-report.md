### Legal Terms And Plain English Pairs

- Visible Light Transmittance (VLT) — the percentage of outside light that passes through the glass and film; higher percent means lighter (more see-through).
- AS-1 line — a mark near the top of the windshield set by the manufacturer; tint is generally allowed only above it (roughly the top 4 inches).
- Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard No. 205 — the federal glass safety rule that sets the 70 percent light minimum California uses.
- Aftermarket tint — film added after you buy the car, as opposed to factory tint built into the glass.
- Sun-screening device — a removable shade (not permanent film) allowed under a medical/visual exemption.
- Correctable violation (fix-it ticket) — a citation you can clear by fixing the problem and showing proof, instead of paying the full fine.
- Luminous transmittance — another way of saying how much light passes through; used in the sun-screening device specs.
- Reflectivity — how mirror-like the film is; California limits this, especially on front windows.
